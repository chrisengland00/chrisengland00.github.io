Deal check

    Cost overrun against sale price

    Profit on this home where construction runs over budget and the sale price moves. Overhead, builder fee and contingency scale with construction cost, so a 10% overrun costs more than 10% of the hard number. Red is a deal where the investor does not get all of their capital back.

    If the market moves

    Sale price held against the same budget. Carry costs and the preferred return keep accruing while the home sits, so the last column shows the same sale price with three more months of marketing.
